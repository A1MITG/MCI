Stage 01 Scope. Turn the user's request into a research plan.

Produce:
- decision: the decision this study informs (use the user's, or propose one and list it as an assumption).
- market_definition, denominator, unit_of_analysis, geography and period, stated precisely.
- evidence_ceiling: what depth the available evidence can support (e.g. "Standard achievable with public web sources only").
- research_questions: 3-6 questions. Each has a question_type from this list and frameworks chosen only from that type's tools:
{{FRAMEWORK_TABLE}}
- competitor_criteria: the explicit criteria used to pick competitors (customer overlap, substitutability, geography, scale, growth, pricing pressure, strategic threat).
- competitors: the competitive set with a one-line reason each, including substitutes or do-nothing where relevant. Never claim the list is exhaustive.
- official_domains: website domains owned by the subject and named competitors (used to tier their official pages).
- assumptions: working assumptions for missing non-essential inputs.
- confirmation: the single most important scoping question for the user, with exactly two short options.
